✦ AI Translator

A little help.

Getting started

Pin AI Translator in Chrome, open it, and sign in with Google. Choose your target language; Ukrainian is selected initially. Enter text or select a phrase on a webpage and click the small translation icon.

Translate a page

Use Translate page in the extension popup. A small panel shows progress and lets you stop translation or restore the original. New page content is translated while this mode is active. A page may contain more text than your remaining monthly allowance; already translated sections stay readable when the allowance runs out.

Translate an image

Right-click a public PNG, JPEG or WebP image on a website and choose Translate image. A new extension tab shows the original, translation progress and a Download PNG button. The current source and target languages are used automatically. Pro is granted manually by the developer; there is no purchase flow. The monthly allowance is five successful images per Free account or 150 per Pro account, resetting on the first day of each calendar month at 00:00 UTC. Input images must be static, at most 10 MB and 20 megapixels. GIF, SVG, data/blob URLs and images requiring website cookies are not supported. AI editing may change visual details, so inspect the result.

Monthly allowance

Free includes 10,000 source words and Pro includes 200,000 source words per account per calendar month, resetting on the first day at 00:00 UTC. Words follow Unicode word boundaries; whitespace, punctuation and standalone emoji do not count. Languages without spaces use Unicode boundaries without dictionary analysis, so individual ideographs can count separately. Successful translated jobs consume the allowance; unsuccessful jobs release their reservation.

Where it works

AI Translator works on ordinary HTTP and HTTPS pages where you have allowed site access. Chrome internal pages, Chrome Web Store pages, the browser's PDF viewer, canvas content and closed Shadow DOM are not supported. Whole-page translation skips image text; image translation is a separate context-menu action. Translation can change line wrapping because languages have different text lengths.

Something went wrong

For a connection error, check your network and retry. If sign-in expires, open the extension and sign in again. If a website continually replaces its content, stop and restart page translation. For further help or a privacy request, use the developer contact on the Chrome Web Store listing. Include the error code and Chrome version, without passwords, API tokens, or private page text.
