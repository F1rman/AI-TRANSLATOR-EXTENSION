A little help understanding a bigger world. Translate a phrase or paragraph with AI in Chrome. Pro also translates eligible webpages.

01 / A phrase

Enter text in the popup, or select text on a webpage and click the translation icon. Copy the result without losing your place.

02 / A whole page

Pro translates eligible page text, including new text during an active session. Pause translation or restore the original whenever you want.

03 / Your context

Pro adds formal, friendly and technical tones. You can choose to include up to 2,000 characters of visible surrounding page text as context.

04 / Local history

Find recent translations on this device. Free shows the latest 20 entries; Pro shows up to 1,000. Delete entries, clear history or turn recording off. There is no cloud history sync.

05 / Your language

Detect the source language automatically, search the language list or enter a language name. The main translation interface is available in English and Ukrainian.

Free: 100,000 source characters per calendar month, with up to 4,000 per translation. The allowance resets on the first day at 00:00 UTC.

Pro: US$4.99 per month or US$39.99 per year, with 2,000,000 source characters per monthly subscription window and up to 50,000 per translation. Annual billing still provides a separate monthly allowance. Pro adds page translation, optional page context, tones and a priority processing queue.

Google sign-in is required. Spaces count toward character usage, and unused allowance does not roll over. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled; cancelling renewal keeps access through the paid period. View Pro plans.
