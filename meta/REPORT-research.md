## Keyword decision

**Primary: webpage translator.** Relevance3, demand3, competition0. The highest demand tier ties several broad phrases; the narrower page task and lowest exact-token competition break the tie. Retain the recognizable AI Translator brand, for example **AI Translator – Webpage Translator**.

Secondary: **translate selected text**, **ai translator**, **full page translator**, **translate webpage**.

Levels are the supplied script's editorial heuristics from current Store top10 user totals plus Google autocomplete. They are **not search volumes, traffic estimates, or a guarantee of rankings**. The script distinguishes “webpage” from “web page”; competition0 does not mean there are no established page translators.

## Sources and limitations

- Raw English probe data: research/keywords-en-round1.json and keywords-en-round2.json. Every record includes its Store source URL, autocomplete suggestions, top10 listings and retrieval time.
- Raw locale probes: research/keywords-{de,fr,it,ru,es}.json. All10locale phrases completed without error.
- One own public-status check: research/own-status.json and own-status-response.html. HTTP200redirected to an empty-title shell; no named listing parsed. The draft state is supplied by the owner conversation.
- Competitor collection: competitors/competitors.json, each competitor's listing.json, description.txt and downloaded images. No listing fetch failures.
- Web open could not access3Store URLs. Direct fetch succeeded(HTTP200); rawHTML retained under research/competitor-{id}.html. The supplied Store parser and visual inspection were used. These recovered sources do not make keyword decisions provisional.
- One asset error: Immersive Translate screenshot1 is a YouTube embed, not an image; left as a recorded download error, other images retained.
- Exact-name collision and visual/positioning notes are editorial judgments based on visible store facts. Ratings and update dates are snapshots, not explanations of product quality.
- No Google Trends comparison or fabricated absolute search-volume data.
- Summed user counts are extension adoption proxies and may double-count people; autocomplete can refer to unrelated platforms/devices.
